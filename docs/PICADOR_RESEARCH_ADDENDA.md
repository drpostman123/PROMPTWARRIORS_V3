# Picador — Research Addenda (three gap-hunting agents, 2026-09-19)

Three agents were tasked to find what `PICADOR_WEBULL_PLAN.md` v1.0 missed. Their full
reports, with sources and raw data pulls, are in `docs/research/` (`webull_api.md`,
`quant.md`, `ops.md`). This file is the synthesis: what changes in the plan, ranked by
impact. Items marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed and are Phase 0 tests.

## A. Corrections that change strategy design

| # | v1.0 said | Now known | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | BTC/ETH hourlies settle on a price print | Settlement is the **simple average of the 60 seconds of CF Benchmarks BRTI/ERTI before HH:00** (contract terms + every market's `rules_primary`) | "Value known within ticks near expiry" is false until ~30 s before close; at 35 % vol BTC's 2-minute sigma ≈ $56 vs a $50 range half-width |
| A2 | S&P 4pm value = official close | Kalshi uses a **real-time ~16:00:00 print** (measured: 7650.12 vs official 7650.50 on 09-18; 7637.71 vs 7637.76 on 09-17). Source Agency is Kalshi itself since Nov 2024; the Google Finance link is "non-binding" | SPXW PM options settle to the official close, so S1's reference must be a live index quote, not the close; 21 % of hourly settlements landed within 0.5 pt of a 5-pt strike |
| A3 | SPY 0DTE chain is good enough for S1 | SPY strikes are $1 = 10 index points vs Kalshi's 5-pt ladder; ATM hourly digital slope is 0.8–1.8 ¢ per index point, so a 10-pt butterfly is 8–18 ¢ wide and 1 bp of SPY/SPX basis noise costs 0.6–1.3 ¢ | **SPX/XSP options data from day one.** Webull's option contract list supports `root_symbol=SPXW`, `underlying_type=INDEX_CALL_OPTION`, `expired_cycle=DAILY`; whether snapshots return index quotes is UNVERIFIED; the OpenAPI options subscription is "OPRA Real-Time Non-display" (price not public) |
| A4 | Split the 0DTE density flat across hours | Measured intraday variance profile (49 days of KXINXU settlements), share of 10:00–16:00 variance by hour: 21/20/7/6/16/30 %; midday hourly sd 12 bp vs 26 bp for 15–16; overnight 16:00→10:00 sd 50 bp; realised vol Jul–Sep 2026 only 8 % ann. | S1 needs a per-hour variance schedule; a flat split misprices the 13:00 contract by ~2.7× in variance |
| A5 | S4 (take 90–98 ¢ in the last minutes) is a near-free edge | Measured markout is **negative**: BTC hourly last-120 s takers at 90–98 ¢ won 93.0 % at avg 93.7 ¢ = −2.6 ¢/contract after fee; 120–300 s: −8.1 ¢; KXINXU 4pm last-120 s: 74 % win = −19.5 ¢; 21,119 contracts bought at ~81 ¢ 2–5 min out won 3.4 %. Cheap-side takers (2–10 ¢) in the last 120 s: **+9.0 ¢**. Makers on the other side: SIG (designated), Jump, Wintermute | **S4 as designed is removed.** Replace with a research item: cheap-side tail buys near expiry, only with the exact resolution feed |
| A6 | S3 range = threshold-difference arbitrage in crypto events | Hourly BTC events contain only two threshold markets (extreme tails) plus 68–186 $100 ranges; live scans: Σ range asks 1.25, Σ bids 0.89, ATM spread 1 ¢, neighbours 3–6 ¢, no executable violation after 2 ¢/leg | S3 stays a scanner (ladder monotonicity on index events, range-sum on crypto); no executor in v1 |
| A7 | Kalshi public REST is enough as second data source | The resolution feed is available **through Kalshi's authenticated WebSocket** (`cfbenchmarks_value` at 1 Hz incl. trailing 60-s average, `cfbenchmarks_value_5hz`) and REST passthrough; needs a free KYC'd Kalshi API key; CF Benchmarks direct requires a licence | Picador needs a **Kalshi account for data** even if it never executes there; and then Kalshi Rule 3.3(b) applies (see C2) |
| A8 | Gates: N ≥ 30/60 trades | At 3 ¢ net edge at 50 ¢ the per-trade Sharpe is 0.06 → t = 2 needs ~1,100 independent trades (5 ¢: 392; 8 ¢: 150). Quarter-Kelly on 3 correlated same-event legs behaves like ¾-Kelly: Monte-Carlo median max drawdown 61 % (p95 81 %) at a true 3 ¢ edge; estimated 5 ¢ / true 0 → 96 % | Gates rewritten in trade counts by price bucket; size per **event** on the worst-case terminal scenario; shrink edge estimates before Kelly (Meister, arXiv 2412.14144) |
| A9 | Liquidity assumed adequate | BTC hourly: median 34,111 contracts / 1,031 trades per hour, only 10–40 of ~188 markets ever trade, 26 % of contracts in the last 5 min. KXINXU median contracts per event: 10:00 171k … 14:00 113k, 16:00 358k; 50 % of the 4pm event trades in the last 30 min; index hourlies open the previous day 16:00 ET and 4.7 % of 4pm volume trades pre-9:30; weekend books 30–45 ¢ wide; early-close days list only H1000–H1300 | Discovery must handle previous-day listing; sizing uses per-strike depth not per-event volume |
| A10 | Deribit gives an hourly BTC density | Deribit public API works unauthenticated (~20 req/s) but the nearest expiry is daily 08:00 UTC (6–30 h out) and ATM quotes are ~20 % wide in premium | S2 must scale the daily density to the hour or use realised-vol; treat S2 as research until the recorder shows an edge |
