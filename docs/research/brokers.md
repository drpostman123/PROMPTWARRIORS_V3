## 1. Robinhood

### (a) Official APIs
- **Crypto Trading API** (launched 2024-05-30) is the only documented REST API: "lets you view crypto market data, access your account information, and place crypto orders programmatically. It's available to Robinhood Crypto customers in the United States." Two versions: v1 (orders without fee tiers), v2 (fee-tier orders; only v2 volume counts toward tiers). Permissions are chosen per credential (read-only vs trading). Source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/crypto-api/ ; docs https://docs.robinhood.com/crypto/trading/
- **Agentic Trading** (announced 2026-05-27, beta): MCP endpoint `https://agent.robinhood.com/mcp/trading`; dedicated "Robinhood Agentic account" funded from the primary account; desktop-only setup; third-party agents (Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, Grok…) explicitly allowed. Asset classes: equities at launch; support page now lists equities and crypto; options "rolling out"; **"options, crypto, event contracts, futures, and more" were "coming soon"** in the launch release and finder.com (Sept 2026) still lists "event contracts, futures and prediction markets are coming, but no specific dates." Sources: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-is-now-open-to-agents/ ; https://robinhood.com/us/en/agentic-trading/ ; https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/agentic-trading-overview/ ; https://www.finder.com/stock-trading/robinhood-agentic-accounts
- **Equities/options/event contracts: no official REST API for retail.** Event contracts are not reachable programmatically today, and prediction markets are mobile-app only ("not available on Robinhood Legend… only available to view on web, not trade"). Source: search summary citing robinhood.com/us/en/prediction-markets/ and support articles.

### (b) Crypto API technicals (from the docs bundle at docs.robinhood.com, extracted from `_next/static/chunks/pages/crypto/trading-*.js`)
- Auth: Ed25519 detached signature over `api_key + timestamp + path + method + body`; headers `x-api-key`, `x-timestamp`, `x-signature`; timestamps expire after 30 s; base `https://trading.robinhood.com`. Source: docs page + https://github.com/nirholas/robinhood-mcp (which also states "Robinhood publishes no sandbox").
- **Rate limits: 100 requests/minute per user account, 300 in bursts, token bucket, applied per endpoint** (docs "Rate Limiting" section).
- Order types: market, limit, stop_loss, stop_limit; TIF enum `gtc, gfd, gfw, gfm`. No websocket documented; no sandbox.
- ToS: Robinhood Terms prohibit "automated searches, requests, or queries to strip, scrape, or mine data"; third-party account access "solely at your risk". Source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/Robinhood%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.pdf (via search).

### (c) Event-contract specifics
- Exchanges: "offered by Robinhood Derivatives, LLC through either KalshiEX LLC, ForecastEX, LLC or Rothera Exchange and Clearing LLC" (support). OG.com (Crypto.com-linked DCM) added as a fourth venue on 2026-09-08 (Axios/Covers). Rothera = Robinhood + SIG JV, DCM+DCO; World Cup and other sports moved to Rothera from June 2026. Sources: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/event-contracts-overview/ ; https://www.axios.com/2026/09/08/robinhood-crypto-og-kalshi-prediction-markets ; https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/the-world-cup-is-now-trading-on-robinhood-and-rothera/
- **Fee (verified, effective 2026-06-01):** Robinhood Derivatives fee schedule: `Total Commission = (k × p × (1 − p) × c)`, k = 10% without Gold, 5% with Gold, "All Commissions are rounded up to the nearest $0.01 increment", "Excludes all applicable Exchange fees"; "Exchange Fees for KalshiEx LLC products offered through Robinhood Derivatives, LLC are $.01 per contract, per side." Support article adds: "We round up to the nearest $0.01 on all trades with a maximum commission rate of $0.01 per contract" and "Exchanges typically charge an additional fee… up to $0.01 per contract… on both opening and closing trades." Worked example in the article: 100 Yes @ $0.90 → $0.45 commission with Gold, $0.90 without. Note the cap: uncapped `0.10·p(1−p)` peaks at 2.5¢ (Gold 1.25¢), so the $0.01 cap binds for p ∈ [0.113, 0.887] non-Gold and p ∈ [0.276, 0.724] Gold; effective all-in cost on Kalshi-routed contracts is therefore ~2¢/side at mid prices and ~1.1–1.5¢ at tails. Sources: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/RHD_Fee_Schedule.pdf (doc id 20260528-5531023) ; https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/event-contracts-overview/
- Rothera exchange fee: `Order fees = MAX(round(k × p × (1 − p) × c, 2), 0.01)`; current k: FCM Retail Customer 0.02, FCM Professional Trading Firm 0.12, Market Maker 0.03 (was 0.000 at the 2026-05-05 self-certification, raised afterwards under Reg 40.6(e)). Sources: https://www.rothera.io/fees ; https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69a0b2d56634068be7fbbf2a/69fa6d5e7f1440875b946a71_20260505%20-%20Rothera%20Fee%20Schedule%20Filing.pdf
- Order types/TIF: IOC ("In contracts"), GTD limit ("until filled or until 3 AM ET the next calendar day"), FOK (Robinhood Learn); sub-penny pricing in some markets (1 decimal, e.g. $0.01–$0.099). Sources: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-event-contracts/ ; https://robinhood.com/us/en/learn/articles/understanding-orders-prices-liquidity-event-contracts/
- Order book: not exposed. "Robinhood shares the same order book as Kalshi, but you do not have visibility on the bid and asks" (third party). Source: https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/robinhood/
- Hours: vary by contract; "often 8 a.m. to 3 a.m. ET" (third party summary of support pages).
- Limits/eligibility: "generally 50,000 contracts" per side per event; Maryland residents cannot trade event contracts; Nevada no new sports contracts (since 2025-12-01); 18+, US resident, individual account with margin enabled or Level 2+ options; separate **Robinhood Derivatives** (FCM) account; instant deposits unavailable; insiders per Kalshi Appendix B barred. Sources: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/event-contracts-restrictions/ ; https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/event-contracts-order-fills/

## 2. tastytrade

### (a) Official API and asset classes
- Open API covers "equities, options, futures, futures options, and cryptocurrency" (developer home, instruments page: Equities, OCC equity options incl. `SPXW 220520C04025000` index options, `/` futures, `./` futures options, `BTC/USD` crypto). **No event-contract instrument type, endpoint, or release note exists** (checked `llms.txt` endpoint index and release notes for "event/predict/kalshi": zero hits). Sources: https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/concepts/instruments-and-symbology ; https://developer.tastytrade.com/llms.txt ; https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/release-notes
- Prediction markets (app/web "Predict" tab) went live 2026-07-27, first firm on Apex Fintech's turnkey FCM; Apex Clearing Corporation segregates event-contract accounts; Apex's own press release of 2026-08-13 describes it as an API platform for *brokers* (AscendOS), not for retail. Categories: commodities, crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE), US/international economics, financials (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Dow, Russell, yields, VIX, DXY); "hourly to yearly timeframes"; 24/7; no sports. Individual margin or cash accounts only, no IRAs. Sources: https://tastytrade.com/prediction-markets/ ; https://apexfintechsolutions.com/press-releases/tastytrade-launches-prediction-markets-giving-active-traders-a-direct-way-to-trade-real-world-market-events/ ; https://apexfintechsolutions.com/press-releases/apex-fintech-solutions-and-kalshi-power-new-era-of-prediction-markets-access/ ; https://www.dimers.com/prediction-markets/legal/tastytrade-launches-cftc-regulated-prediction-markets ; https://tastytrade.com/learn/trading-products/other-products/prediction-markets/
- **Answer to (e): Apex/Kalshi prediction markets are NOT reachable via the tastytrade Open API today.** Order entry is in the Predict tab (Market, Dollar Amount, Limit).

### (b) Auth, sandbox, streaming, limits, ToS
- Auth: OAuth2 only (legacy `POST /sessions` decommissioned 2026-02-11). Create OAuth app at my.tastytrade.com (scopes `read`, `trade`, `openid`), create a personal grant → non-expiring refresh token → 15-minute JWT access tokens via `POST /oauth/token`; `User-Agent: product/version` header mandatory. Personal apps are restricted to your own account. Source: https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/authentication/oauth2
- Sandbox: `https://api.cert.tastyworks.com` + `wss://streamer.cert.tastyworks.com`; separate credentials; fills are synthetic (market always fills at $1; limit < $3 fills immediately; ≥ $3 never fills); market-data routes return 502; environment resets every 24 h. Source: https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/sandbox
- Streaming: DXLink (`wss://tasty-openapi-ws.dxfeed.com/realtime`, 24-h API quote token) with hard caps: 5 concurrent sessions, 25,000 subscriptions/session, 100 each for Candle/Order/Series, 10,000 subscription changes/min; account streamer `wss://streamer.tastyworks.com`. Sources: https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/concepts/streaming ; https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/guides/rate-limits-and-backoff
- REST rate limits: **unpublished** "reasonable thresholds"; bare 429 with no headers; ~8 h IP block after repeated failed logins; the tastytrade MCP server uses a 50 req/s global cap as a reference ceiling. Same source.
- Orders: Limit/Market/Stop etc.; TIF `Day`, `GTC`, `GTD` (+ IOC/extended-hours on some venues); complex OTOCO/OCO/OTO/PAIRS; dry-run endpoint; **submission is not idempotent** (use `external-identifier`). Source: https://developer.tastytrade.com/docs/concepts/orders-and-order-types
- API Terms of Service (PDF, last updated 2023-05-17): autotrading is contemplated ("A third-party integrator shall be permitted to buy, sell or otherwise trade on behalf of a customer… enrolled in the Autotrading services"); integrators may not store credentials for "automated or 'easy' authentication"; no redistribution of data; user bears "errant order instructions sent as a result of software malfunction"; breach lets tastytrade "bust orders previously entered and filled via the API Connection". Source: https://assets.tastyworks.com/production/documents/USA/open_api_terms_and_conditions.pdf
- Commissions (hedge legs): stock/ETF options $1/contract to open ($10 cap/leg), $0 to close; broad-based index options $1 to open, $0 to close; futures $1.25/side; micro futures $0.75; crypto 1% (BTC/ETH 0.75%). Source: https://tastytrade.com/pricing/
- Event-contract fees on tastytrade: **UNVERIFIED** (help article "Kalshi Event Contracts" and "Commissions and Fees Overview" render only a CSS error to fetchers; pricing page lists no event-contract line). Kalshi's own `0.07·P·(1−P)` schedule is referenced in tastytrade material.

### SDKs

## 4. Kalshi direct

### (a)/(b) API
- REST + WebSocket + FIX; RSA-PSS signatures (`KALSHI-ACCESS-KEY/-TIMESTAMP/-SIGNATURE`), private key shown once. Demo: `https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2`, `wss://external-api-ws.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/ws/v2`, separate credentials. Sources: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_keys ; https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env
- Rate tiers (tokens/s read/write): Basic 200/100, Advanced 300/300 (self-serve via "Upgrade Account API Usage Level"), Expert 600/600, Premier 1,000, Paragon 2,000, Prime 4,000, Prestige 10,000/8,000; Expert+ earned by 30-day volume share (0.075%…1.0% of 2× prior-month exchange volume) or assigned. Most calls cost 10 tokens. No API fee. Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits
- **Create Order v2 requires `self_trade_prevention_type`: `taker_at_cross` (cancel the incoming order when it would trade against your own resting order; partial fills already matched stand) or `maker` (cancel your resting order and continue matching).** Also `post_only`, `reduce_only`, `cancel_order_on_pause`, `expiration_time` (GTC only), `subaccount`, `order_group_id`, TIF `fill_or_kill | good_till_canceled | immediate_or_cancel`. Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/orders/create-order-v2
- Fees: taker `round-up(0.07 × C × P × (1−P))`, maker `0.0175 × C × P × (1−P)` (series multipliers may differ, e.g. crypto/index hourlies list `quadratic` multiplier 1); no settlement or membership fee; the basis-point volume tiers (12.0 → 2.6 bps) apply to **perpetuals**, not event contracts. Sources: https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf (7/7/26 update; blocked by Vercel checkpoint to fetchers today) via https://www.botforkalshi.com/blog/kalshi-fees-explained and https://predictreport.io/blog/kalshi-fees-explained ; https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823805-fees
- Direct account: one account per person ("automatically deny any such applications beyond the first account"). Source: https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823781-can-i-have-multiple-kalshi-accounts

### (g) Rulebook v1.29 (verbatim extracts; file `kalshi_rulebook_v129.txt`)
- **Rule 3.3(b):** "FCM Customers are allowed to have accounts with multiple FCMs. However, an FCM Customer must not intentionally match its own orders against each other. If an FCM Customer places orders that match each other, that may constitute a trading violation and subject the FCM Customer to discipline under these Rules, as well as violate the CEA and CFTC Regulations. **Before an FCM Customer trades on Kalshi with multiple accounts, it must disclose the identity of each account to the Exchange by email (or as otherwise specified by Kalshi in lieu of sending an email).**" (The `rule33@kalshi.com` address quoted in the addenda is not in the v1.29 text; the rule says "by email (or as otherwise specified)".)
- **Rule 3.3(c):** a Self-Clearing Member (direct account) who is also an FCM Customer must "inform Kalshi of the FCM that it is a Customer of via email" and "not intentionally match its own orders against each other."
- **Rule 3.3(e):** "An FCM Customer must adhere to all relevant Position Limits. This includes if the FCM trades through multiple FCMs or IBs, or is also a Self-Clearing Member."
- **Rule 5.19(f):** position limits apply to "All positions in accounts for which any Person by power of attorney or otherwise, directly or indirectly holds positions or controls trading; and… Positions held by two or more Persons acting pursuant to an express or implied agreement or understanding the same as if the positions were held by… a single Person." 5.19(e): FCMs must report all FCM Customers above Reportable Levels. 5.19(a): bids/offers that *would* breach a limit if accepted are themselves violations.
- **Rule 5.17 Prohibited Transactions:** (b) no non-competitive / prearranged trades ("a Trader may not agree in advance with another Trader that one… will enter an Order and the other… will attempt to trade against that Order by timing the submission"); (c) no trade that "Does not result in a change in beneficial ownership", "Is designed to unnaturally inflate trading volume", or circumvents competitive exposure; (aa) spoofing, violating bids/offers, reckless disregard during the close; (bb) "money pass", "wash trade", "front-running"; (n) manipulation under CEA 6(c)/9(a)(2).
- **Rule 5.11(c)(v)/(d):** $3,000 paid by the Trader whose order caused a cancellation/adjustment; the "automated trading system malfunction" extraordinary-circumstance exception is available only to Self-Clearing Members who are market makers or "have been granted access to an advanced tier of Kalshi's API" — never to FCM customers.
- **Rule 5.12:** suspension/revocation of an FCM cancels all of that FCM's customer orders and invalidates subsequent trades.
- **Rule 3.8(e)/(f):** FCMs must ensure customers adhere to position limits, run "automated pre-trade risk controls", and for individual customers "restrict access to any system through which such individual FCM Customer submits Orders… to that individual FCM Customer" (i.e., you, not a third party, must be the one operating each broker account).
- **Rule 3.6(a)/(f):** respond to information requests within 15 days; "maintain appropriate books and records of its trading, including records of any activity in the underlying commodity and related derivatives markets" (relevant to options/crypto hedge legs).
- Source: https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/regulatory/rulebook/Kalshi%20DCM%20Rulebook%20v.1.29.pdf

## 7. Design question: one beneficial owner, several bots, one Kalshi book

### 7.1 Constraints (with rule cites)
1. **Multi-account disclosure before trading (Kalshi Rule 3.3(b), (c)).** Trading Kalshi through Webull *and* IBKR *and* a direct Kalshi key is allowed only after disclosing every account to Kalshi; a direct account holder who is also an FCM customer must additionally name the FCM(s). Rothera Rule 2.5(D) is word-for-word the same for Rothera (relevant only via Robinhood, which has no API). Sources: rulebook v1.29; Rothera DCM Rulebook 2026-05-20 https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69a0b2d56634068be7fbbf29/6a0cccc17b4fec097e1ab02c_20260520%20-%20Rothera%20DCM%20Rulebook_Clean.pdf
2. **No self-matching, no wash trades, no prearranged legs (Kalshi 3.3(b), 5.17(b),(c),(bb); Rothera 5.x(G) "wash sales"; CEA §4c(a)(2)(A) 7 U.S.C. 6c; 17 CFR 38.152).** Two of your bots resting on opposite sides of the same Kalshi market through different FCMs *will* eventually match. Only the direct-Kalshi route exposes `self_trade_prevention_type`, and it only knows about orders under that Kalshi member — it cannot see your Webull or IBKR orders. Therefore the orchestrator must run a **global cross-venue pre-trade check**: a single registry of every resting order and position per Kalshi ticker across all brokers; never send an order that could cross your own resting order on the same book (same ticker, opposite side, price ≥ your resting price); cancel-then-place with confirmation before flipping sides; one "owner" venue per (ticker, side) at any time. Intent matters for discipline, but Rule 5.17(c) ("does not result in a change in beneficial ownership") is strict-liability in form. Sources: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/7/6c ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/38.152
3. **Position limits and accountability aggregate across all your accounts (Kalshi 3.3(e), 5.19(f); Rothera 7.17; 17 CFR 150.4(a)).** Not binding at Picador size (INX $7M/member, BTC $1M/strike per addenda), but 5.19(e) reportable levels are reported per FCM and Kalshi's surveillance will aggregate by identity; keep a consolidated position ledger so you can answer a Rule 3.6(a) information request within 15 days. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/150.4
4. **Error-trade exposure is per venue and uncapped for FCM customers (Kalshi 5.11):** a fat finger on any of the bots costs $3,000 plus adjustment; the "automated system malfunction" carve-out is only for advanced-tier/market-maker *direct* members. Price-band and notional guards must sit in the orchestrator, upstream of every broker adapter.
5. **Each broker account must be operated by you (Kalshi 3.8(f) for individuals; Webull ToS; Public "solely for Developer's own personal use", no mirroring; tastytrade ToS autotrading permitted but you own errant orders; Robinhood: no sanctioned API for events, unofficial use → account freeze (#1604)).** Running one orchestrator under your own credentials on your own infrastructure is consistent with all of these; letting a third party operate an account is not.
6. **Rule 5.12 venue-suspension risk is real and asymmetric:** if an FCM (e.g., Webull, under state sports enforcement) is suspended, all its customer orders are cancelled and later trades voided. Multiple FCMs are genuine redundancy against *FCM* failure, not against a Kalshi outage or a Kalshi market "under review".
7. **Books and records / tax:** Rule 3.6(f) requires records of related-market activity (your SPX/XSP/crypto hedges); multiple FCMs means multiple 1099 streams and no cross-broker wash-sale accounting — the journal must consolidate fills from every venue.
8. **NFA:** NFA rules bind the FCMs (Kalshi 3.8(m)), not a retail customer; your obligations flow through the exchange rulebook, the CEA/CFTC anti-fraud and anti-manipulation provisions, and the broker agreements. (NFA assessment fees appear on futures, not event contracts — RHD fee schedule footnote.)

### 7.2 What legitimately remains
- **Fee routing (same Kalshi book, different fee schedules):** IBKR $0.02 flat (= Webull); Kalshi direct taker `0.07·P(1−P)` (≤ 1.75¢, 0.35¢ at 5¢) and maker `0.0175·P(1−P)` (≤ 0.44¢) — the only route where resting/maker strategies are viable; Robinhood at the tails (~1.1–1.5¢ all-in) but no API; tastytrade unknown. Rule: send *takers* to whichever of {Webull, IBKR, Kalshi-direct} is cheapest at that price, send *makers* only to Kalshi-direct, and never quote both sides across venues.
- **Different exchanges = different books, so no self-match risk and genuine cross-venue arbitrage:** IBKR gives API access to ForecastEx ($0.01 exchange fee, buy-only, coupon-bearing) and CME event contracts alongside Kalshi, with IBKR's own best-net-price router; the IBKR Campus article notes "spreads across the venues can vary significantly, offering potential arbitrage possibilities." Robinhood's Rothera/OG.com/ForecastEx flow is app-only today (MCP event support "coming soon").
- **Hedge-leg asset classes:** tastytrade (SPX/SPXW/XSP options via OCC symbols, /ES /MES futures, crypto; DXLink data), IBKR (everything incl. SPX/XSP, futures, crypto), Public (index options $0.35 + fees, crypto), Alpaca (equity options L1–3, crypto, paper), Robinhood Crypto API (crypto only, 100 req/min). Webull's SPX data remains UNVERIFIED per addenda, so tastytrade or IBKR is the practical S1 options-data source.
- **Redundancy:** independent FCMs (Rule 5.12), independent API rate budgets (Webull 600 orders/min, IBKR 50 req/s, Kalshi 100–300 write tokens/s, tastytrade unpublished, Public 10 req/s), independent auth failure modes.
- **Sandboxes for the same code path:** Webull paper, tastytrade cert (no market data, synthetic fills), Kalshi demo, Alpaca paper, IBKR paper (event contracts UNVERIFIED); Public and Robinhood have none.

### 7.3 Practical implications for the five-bot plan
- Of the five requested bots, **only Webull, Kalshi direct and (with Phase-0 verification) IBKR can trade event contracts by API today.** Robinhood and tastytrade event contracts are app-only; Public has none. Robinhood's crypto API and tastytrade's Open API remain useful as hedge-leg/data venues.
- Before the second event-capable account trades: email Kalshi under Rule 3.3(b) listing every account (Webull Event Contract account id, IBKR account, Kalshi member id), keep the acknowledgement, and re-notify when adding IBKR/Alpaca later.
- Architect the orchestrator so that one process owns the cross-venue order registry and issues every order token; broker bots are executors, not decision-makers, or the anti-self-match guarantee is unenforceable.
